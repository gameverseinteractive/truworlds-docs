# TruWorlds Creator

TruWorlds Creator is the application you use to build, script, test and publish worlds.

## The window

- Its [window](interface-overview.md) is four bands, from top to bottom: the menu bar, the ribbon, the workspace and the status bar.
- The [viewport](viewport-and-selection.md) is the 3D view in the center of TruWorlds Creator, on the first tab.
- The [ribbon](ribbon-and-insert.md) is the band of buttons under the menu bar.
- To [transform](transform-tools.md) an object is to change its position, orientation or size.
- The [Explorer](explorer.md) is the panel that shows everything in a world as a tree of its instances.
- The [Properties panel](properties-panel.md) shows the properties of whatever is selected.

## Assets, scripts and UI

- The [Asset Library](asset-library.md) holds meshes, images, sounds, animations and prefabs stored in your TruWorlds account.
- The [Asset Browser](asset-browser.md) is the panel that shows the files stored inside your world.
- The [script editor](script-editor.md) is the code editor built into TruWorlds Creator.
- The [Script Console](script-console.md) is the panel that shows messages, including a log of what TruWorlds Creator itself did.
- The [UI editor](ui-editor.md) lets you click, drag and resize a world's UI elements right in the viewport.

## Settings and agents

[Settings](settings.md) lists its categories, General and Agents, on the left, and the chosen category's options on the right. [Connect agent](ai-agents.md), on the right side of the ribbon's tab row, opens the Connect an agent window.
